E! Nkulunkulu Nkosi

zu: E! Nkulunkulu Nkosi en: O God, Lord
verse 1
E! Nkulunkulu Nkosi, zivume lezizipho, O God, Lord, accept these gifts
Ezinikelwa kuwe nguMpriste elathini. Offered to you by the priest at the altar
Awenze ziphenduke Inyama yoMsindisi, Make them become the Body of the Saviour
Negazi lakhe futhi kub' umhlabiso omuhle. And His Blood too, a beautiful offering
verse 2
Wubheke lomhlabiso, usihawukele ngawo Look upon this offering and have mercy on us through it
Nalabo abahlanzwa izono emlilweni. And on those being cleansed of sins in the fire
Ungab' usazibheka izono zethu manje, Do not look upon our sins now
Njengoba sinxuselwa nguJesu elathini. As we are pleaded for by Jesus at the altar
